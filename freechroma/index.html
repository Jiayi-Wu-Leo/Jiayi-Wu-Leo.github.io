Generative
chroma keying

FreeCHROMA adapts a pretrained video diffusion model in a parameter-efficient manner for generative video compositing from one to three unaligned background references.

The model integrates the foreground into the referenced environment, preserving foreground identity, motion, and fine visual details while generating a spatiotemporally coherent background.

Foreground–background harmonization occurs within the same generation process, without a separate relighting stage. Optional text instructions help disambiguate complex spatial relationships and camera behavior.

How it works

We build training pairs from natural videos and use them to adapt a pretrained video diffusion model for joint composition and harmonization.

Training data

We construct pseudo-pairs from natural videos by perturbing foreground illumination and creating clean background references with altered viewpoints. The original video remains the training target.

Model

Using these pairs, we train added conditioning layers and LoRA adapters while keeping the pretrained weights frozen. Foreground, mask, and background-reference inputs guide composition and harmonization in one denoising process.

Ablation study

Without foreground illumination perturbationThe model tends to retain the source foreground lighting and color casts.

Without background viewpoint augmentationThe model tends to inherit the unaligned reference viewpoint, producing inconsistent scene geometry.
